About DoctorConnect

Open, Independent Public Healthcare Directory

Zero paywalls, zero login restrictions. Connecting patients with verified medical care in Bangladesh.

BMDC Verification Commitment

Every doctor featured on DoctorConnect holds a verified registration with the Bangladesh Medical & Dental Council (BMDC). Patients can inspect BMDC numbers directly on each profile to verify legitimacy.

Why No Admin Login or Middleman?

Traditional healthcare portals frequently force patients to create accounts, pay commissions, or go through slow customer support desks just to find out a doctor's chamber serial number or visiting days.

DoctorConnect was engineered as a controlled data-import and public-service directory:

  • Direct Serial Numbers: Call the chamber or hospital desk directly to book your appointment without intermediary fees.
  • Transparent Consultation Fees: View initial consultation fees, follow-up fees, and report check fees upfront.
  • Day-Wise Visiting Hours: Filter doctors by who is actually sitting in the chamber today.
  • Developer & Community Managed: Maintained through robust CSV/JSON data pipelines and public doctor registration submissions.
